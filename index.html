About Atay Balunan Store

Atay Balunan Store is a local store in Macau offering Filipino products, snacks, grocery essentials, and health and personal-care items.

We aim to make familiar Filipino products and everyday necessities accessible to our community.

We also provide Padala services between Macau and the Philippines, helping people stay connected with their loved ones.

Whether you're looking for Filipino favorites or everyday essentials, you're welcome at Atay Balunan Store!
